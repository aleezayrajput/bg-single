Our story

A gate to the city's favourite flavours

The Burger Gate takes its name and its look from the grand gateways of old Lahore. Every guest who walks in should feel like they are arriving somewhere special, and leave full.

We started with burgers and kept adding what our guests asked for. Today one kitchen turns out burgers, pizza, BBQ, Chinese and fast food, plus drinks and desserts to finish.
